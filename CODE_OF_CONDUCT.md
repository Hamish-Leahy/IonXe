Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to fostering an open and welcoming environment.

Our Pledge
In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassment of any kind
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment

Enforcement
Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers via security contact in `SECURITY.md`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
